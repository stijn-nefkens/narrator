Library QoL pass.

- Swipe a book to the left to delete it. A confirmation dialog asks
  before anything is removed; tapping Cancel snaps the row back into
  place.
- Long-press now enters selection mode and ticks that row in one
  motion. No more intermediate "Edit / Select" menu. Tap other books
  to add them to the selection, tap × in the toolbar (or back) to exit.
- Selected rows now show a clear coloured background instead of the
  old alpha-dim trick that was effectively invisible.
- A pen icon appears in the action-mode toolbar when exactly one book
  is selected — tap it to open the Edit details dialog. Disappears
  when 2+ books are selected (where editing doesn't make sense).
- "Select all" added to the action mode overflow menu.
- Search field gets a × button to clear the query.
- Each book row now shows a small EPUB / PDF chip so you can tell at
  a glance what kind of source you're looking at.
