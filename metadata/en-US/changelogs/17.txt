Better Roman numerals and table handling in PDFs.

- Roman numerals in ordinary text are now read as numbers:
  "Louis XIV" → "Louis 14", "World War II" → "World War 2". The
  pronoun "I" and common abbreviations (CD, DC, MIX…) are left
  alone.
- Tables in PDFs are detected and skipped more reliably, including
  shorter tables and tightly-set numeric tables (years and figures
  in columns) that previously got read out row by row.
