Player + Settings polish.

Player
- Speed control consolidated into a single chip. Tap = reset to 1.0x;
  horizontal drag = adjust in 0.1x steps; long-press = open a Material slider
  for explicit selection. Replaces the previous -/+ buttons.
- Sleep timer now freezes when you pause and resumes from where it left off
  when you press play, instead of running through pause time.
- Sleep, 1.00x, and Mark chips share a uniform width for a balanced row.
- Cover-tap to play/pause; tap the chapter line to jump to any chapter.
- Bookmark dialog: long-press a row to delete.

Settings
- Theme is a segmented button row [System | Light | Dark | Black] instead of
  stacked radios.
- "Continue through chapter boundaries" removed (narrator always continues).
- Tap anywhere on a settings row to toggle the switch — consistent with the
  voice-setup engine rows.
- Voice setup engine rows no longer light up their background; the switch is
  the sole indication of which engine is active.

Robustness
- Speed +/- bug: previous version got stuck at 1.05 because of float drift.
  All step math now snaps to the 0.1 grid.
- Bookmark sanity-clamp verified on re-imports with different chunking.
