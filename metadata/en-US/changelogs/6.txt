PDF support.

You can now import PDFs alongside EPUBs. The picker in the Library
accepts .pdf files, and the system "Open with" menu lists Narrator
for PDFs from your file manager.

Text-extraction quality fixes that make PDF narration listenable:

- Front-matter: copyright pages, tables of contents, references,
  bibliographies, indexes, and glossaries are skipped automatically.
- Running headers, footers, and page numbers are stripped per page.
- Words split across a line break by an end-of-line hyphen are rejoined.
- Soft hyphens (invisible characters used for justified text) are
  removed so they don't become audible syllable breaks.
- Ligatures (ﬁ ﬂ ﬃ etc.) are normalised to plain letters.
- Bullet glyphs at the start of lines are stripped.
- Common abbreviations are expanded so the narrator says "Doctor"
  instead of "dee", "for example" instead of "ee gee", etc.
- Roman numerals in chapter headings ("Chapter XIV") are converted to
  Arabic so they don't get spelled letter-by-letter.
- URLs and email addresses become spoken placeholders ("link", "email
  address") instead of getting spelled out character-by-character.
- Image, figure, and table captions are skipped.
- Tables (detected by repeated column-aligned rows) are dropped so
  they don't get read cell-by-cell.
- Footnotes (text in smaller-than-body font) are dropped.

Chapters come from the PDF's table-of-contents when present, or are
inferred from font-size jumps for PDFs that don't have one.

Note: ~12 MB APK size increase from the PDFBox-Android library.
