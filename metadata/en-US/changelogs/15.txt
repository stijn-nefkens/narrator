Faster book switching and one more text fix.

- Switching between recently-read books is now near-instant: the
  app keeps the parsed text of your last few unfinished books ready
  in the background, so re-opening one skips the loading wait.
- Words accidentally glued together (e.g. "morningCame" from PDF
  text extraction) are now split so the voice pronounces both
  words. Brand and surname forms like iPhone, McDonald and
  MacArthur are deliberately left intact.
