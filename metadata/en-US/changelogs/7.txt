PDF import escape hatches.

When the automatic cleanup heuristics don't get a PDF quite right, you
now have three ways to take over:

Page range at import
- When importing a PDF, you're asked which pages to include. Leave the
  fields blank to import all, or specify a start and end page to crop
  out front matter / appendices the heuristics miss.

Import preview
- After parsing, a dialog lists the chapters with the first ~160
  characters of body text per chapter. Useful for spotting a PDF that
  extracted as garbage (image-only / weird fonts) before it lands in
  your library. Tap "Add to library" or back out.

Per-book skip patterns
- Long-press a book in the library and tap "Edit details" — a new
  "Skip patterns" field lets you enter one regex per line. Any chunk
  matching a pattern is dropped at load time. Useful for recurring
  cruft a single book ("^Continued on page", "^DRAFT —", etc.).

The selected page range and skip patterns are persisted per book and
re-applied every time the source is re-parsed.
