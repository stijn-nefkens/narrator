Playback reliability, faster opening, and a tidier reading flow.

- Books no longer get stuck "synthesising" — if the voice engine stalls,
  playback now recovers on its own instead of needing a manual skip.
- Opening a large PDF is fast again, even on a cold start: the parsed
  text is cached to disk, so re-opening a book no longer re-parses it.
- The notification progress bar now fills across the current chapter
  instead of inching forward per sentence.
- Inline references like "(Smith, 2019)", "(see fig 1.3)" and "[12]"
  are skipped when reading, so citations no longer interrupt the flow.
- Fixed a leftover click on sentences ending in patterns like ".).".
- Finished books stay greyed out after you select and deselect them.
- Library and Player buttons swapped; pressing Back on the Player now
  returns to the Library instead of closing the app.
