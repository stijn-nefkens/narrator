Reading-quality improvements.

- Playback now pauses when you unplug headphones or disconnect a
  Bluetooth speaker, instead of switching to the phone speaker.
- Inline references like "(Smith, 2020; Jones, 2018)" are now removed,
  including lists of several citations in one set of brackets.
- Figure and table captions ("Figure 4.2 The map of ...") are skipped
  more reliably, while sentences that merely refer to a figure are kept.
- Names with initials like "J.H. Blom" are no longer split mid-name.
- The author name is now centered on the player screen.

Note: each book re-parses once on first open after this update.
