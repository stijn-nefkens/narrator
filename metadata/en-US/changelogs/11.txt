Sentence chunking + backup fixes.

- Sentences where the source text dropped the space after a period
  ("scale.Quite", "abroad.As") were being treated as one mega-sentence
  by the sentence-splitter, which then hit the 500-char hard cap and
  cut mid-word ("touc | hing"). Sentences.split now restores the
  missing space before splitting. Applies to EPUB and PDF.
- Sub-chunker now searches the whole sentence for clause breaks
  instead of giving up after 150 characters. A long sentence whose
  first comma/dash sits past 150 chars is now split at that break
  instead of falling back to a hard cut. Cost function still biases
  toward target ~70-char chunks.
- Backup: PRAGMA wal_checkpoint(FULL) was being run via execSQL,
  which rejects statements that return rows. Switched to rawQuery
  with cursor consumption.
