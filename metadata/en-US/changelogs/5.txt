Fixes a runaway-playback bug when the TTS engine is unavailable.

If the selected TTS engine (e.g. sherpa-onnx) is disabled, uninstalled,
or otherwise can't be bound, every chunk's synthesis fails instantly.
Previously this looked like playback was racing ahead at ~10x: the
position counter advanced for each failed chunk while no audio was
rendered.

- After three consecutive synth failures, the pipeline now halts and
  pauses playback instead of advancing through the book.
- The Player shows a brief notice with a one-tap shortcut to Voice
  setup so the engine can be fixed.
- Single isolated synth failures still skip past the bad chunk so a
  weird sentence doesn't stop the whole book.
