Smoother narration and cleaner text.

- Loading spinner when opening a book, so a large PDF no longer
  looks frozen while it parses.
- Numbers like "200,000" are now read as a single number instead
  of being mangled by the grouping commas.
- Sentences are cut shorter and more of them are buffered ahead,
  so the voice keeps up in real time and transitions are smoother.
- Sentences with a parenthetical aside in the middle now break
  cleanly around the brackets when they're long.
- Better detection of sentences glued together by a missing space
  after a period — including after numbers and quotes.
