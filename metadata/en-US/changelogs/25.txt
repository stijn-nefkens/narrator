Fixes playback regressions from 0.19.0.

- Playback no longer stalls or stutters: the new stuck-synthesis safety
  net was triggering during normal reading. It now only steps in for a
  genuinely hung voice engine.
- Long sentences are split into smaller pieces again, so the voice keeps
  up instead of pausing to synthesise a big block.
- The notification progress bar no longer sits at 100% — it now tracks
  your position within the current chapter.
