QoL pass: 10 small improvements.

Library
- Mark a book as finished (toggle in Edit details). Finished books get a
  small "FINISHED" chip and are slightly dimmed so the eye gravitates
  to in-progress ones.
- Reset progress button in Edit details: confirms, then puts the book
  back at chapter 1. Named bookmarks are kept.
- Empty library now shows a prominent "Import a book" button below the
  hint text — not just a wall of words for first-time use.
- Long-press a second book while in selection mode to extend the
  selection between the two rows (shift-click style).

Player
- First-time tooltip explains the speed-chip gestures (tap to reset,
  drag to step, long-press for slider). One-shot, never shows again.
- Sleep timer fades out the audio over the last 15 seconds instead of
  cutting mid-sentence.
- Long-press a chapter in the chapter navigator to bookmark its
  starting position instead of jumping to it.

Audio
- Quiet, brief interruptions (notification chimes) now duck the
  narrator's volume to ~30% and restore it instead of pausing. Phone
  calls and longer interruptions still pause cleanly.

Settings
- After backup, a Snackbar appears with a Share action to send the ZIP
  to another app (cloud, email, etc.) in one tap.
- Backup filename now includes hour-minute so multiple same-day
  backups don't collide.
