Progress accuracy, chapter titles, and import flow.

- Finished books now show 100% (and are auto-marked finished when you
  reach the end), instead of sticking at 99%.
- Fixed wrong progress percentages on books imported before the 0.16
  update — their resume positions are reset once so the percentage is
  accurate again. (Affected books restart from the beginning.)
- Chapter titles are no longer glued onto the first sentence; the title
  is read on its own with a short pause before the chapter body.
- A loading spinner now shows when you open a book and when you import
  one, so a slow parse no longer looks like nothing happened.
- Importing a book is now one tap — the preview step was removed.
- Removed the separate "export bookmarks" button; bookmarks are already
  included when you back up your library in Settings.
