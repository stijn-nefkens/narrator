More natural narration — fewer mid-sentence cuts.

- Sentences are no longer chopped into short fixed-length pieces.
  When there's enough audio buffered ahead, a whole sentence is read
  as a single natural utterance, so the voice's intonation stays
  intact. Cutting only kicks in for the very first sentence after you
  press play (to start the audio quickly) or if the engine briefly
  falls behind. Long sentences that used to sound choppy now read
  smoothly.
