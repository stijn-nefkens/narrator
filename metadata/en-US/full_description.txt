Narrator is an audiobook player for EPUB and PDF files. Designed for /e/OS and
de-Googled Android devices, but works on any Android 8.0+ phone.

Features

- Import EPUB and PDF files via the system file picker, share menu, or by
  tapping them in any file manager.
- Plays through the on-device TextToSpeech engine of your choice. Sherpa-onnx
  with Kokoro is recommended for natural neural narration; a one-tap "Install
  natural voice" button downloads it directly from the official mirror.
- Background playback with lock-screen and notification controls. Auto-pauses
  on phone calls and other audio focus loss.
- Mini-player above the bottom nav so you can pause / skip from any tab.
- Speed control: tap to reset to 1.0x, drag to adjust in 0.1x steps, long-press
  for a slider. Per-book speed memory plus a global default.
- Per-book bookmarks (with plain-text export), resume position, sleep timer
  (end-of-chapter, 15/30/60 minutes, or custom).
- Scrub bar with chapter-boundary dots.
- Follow-along text with word-by-word highlighting.
- Library: search, four sort modes, multi-select, swipe-to-delete with undo,
  per-book rename and skip-patterns editor.
- Backup the whole library (DB + books + covers) to a single ZIP, restore on
  another device.
- Themes: light, dark, follow system, true-black AMOLED.

PDF cleanup

The PDF parser drops front-matter (copyright, TOC), back-matter (references,
index, glossary), running headers/footers, page numbers, image captions, and
table regions. It rejoins hyphenated line breaks, normalises ligatures and
soft hyphens, expands common abbreviations, converts Roman numerals in
chapter headings, and replaces URLs with spoken placeholders. For PDFs where
the heuristics fall short, you can pick a page range at import time, preview
the parsed chapters before committing, and add per-book regex skip patterns.

Privacy

Everything runs on the device. No network access, no analytics, no tracking,
no cloud sync. Your books, bookmarks, and progress never leave the phone.
The one exception is the "Install natural voice" button — it opens the Kokoro
APK download URL in your browser, but only when you explicitly tap it.
