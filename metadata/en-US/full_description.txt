Narrator is an audiobook player for EPUB files. Designed for /e/OS and de-Googled
Android devices, but works on any Android 8.0+ phone.

Features

- Import EPUB files via the system file picker; the parser strips page numbers and
  other non-narratable artefacts, then splits the text into clause-aware chunks.
- Plays through the on-device TextToSpeech engine of your choice. Sherpa-onnx /
  Kokoro is recommended for natural neural narration; RHVoice is a lighter fallback.
- Background playback with lock-screen / notification controls.
- Per-book bookmarks, resume position, playback speed memory.
- Sleep timer (end-of-chapter, 15/30/60 minutes).
- Follow-along text with word-by-word highlighting.

Privacy

Everything runs on the device. No network access, no analytics, no tracking, no
cloud sync. Your books, bookmarks, and progress never leave the phone.
